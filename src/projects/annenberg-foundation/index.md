# {{ title }}

> The Annenberg Foundation is a family foundation established in 1989. The Foundation supports the worldwide community through its grantmaking, technical assistance and direct charitable activities.

I worked with [Advomatic](https://www.advomatic.com) to develop the new site for the Annenberg Foundation where I was responsible for front-end development and theming.

## About the project

For the Annenberg Foundation's new website, Advomatic handled the Drupal build and I handled the front end. I wrote the custom Drupal theme in HTML, CSS and jQuery and themed the sections shown below: the home page, Annenberg Now, Who We Are and Initiatives. I also consulted on front-end decisions throughout the project.

* Drupal Theme

* Consulting
